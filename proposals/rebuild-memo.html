Reality check

This is probably the wrong project for this client

Batshi Home is a wholesale-priced home-goods SMB. The headless-on-Woo path already delivered gives ~80% of the customer-facing upside — speed, design, mobile, SEO, discovery — for ~₪80k all-in, keeps a battle-tested admin, and keeps the whole WooCommerce plugin ecosystem doing its job. From-scratch is ~6.5× the cost, ~5× the timeline, and hands the client (and you) a bespoke admin, a bespoke pricing engine, and a bespoke shipping engine to own and patch forever.

Only pitch this if the client has a concrete reason Woo cannot continue: a licensing / cost ceiling they have actually hit, a performance wall the headless front end did not solve, a business model Woo genuinely cannot represent, or an investor / acquirer requiring fully owned IP. "It would be cleaner" is not a reason worth ₪520k of their money.

Anchor
Open at ₪520,000, itemized + milestone plan. Do not present a lump sum — the client must see it is 15 distinct builds, not a website.
Floor ₪400k
Reachable only by cutting: swap the custom admin for Medusa / Saleor (−₪80–100k but you lose "fully owned"), drop order-history migration (−₪12k), defer the promotions engine to post-launch (−₪18k), shorten hypercare.
Do not solo blind
10 months of solo delivery risk is high — one illness or one hard client and the timeline doubles. Price a second developer into M3 (commerce core) or extend the schedule and say so in writing.
Highest-risk line
Pricing + shipping engine. You are replacing Advanced Woo Discount Rules, ELEX Flexible Pricing, PI SOL shipping, and Smart Coupons. Get every rule documented and signed before M3 starts; the contingency lives here.
Payment dependency
Grow / Meshulam headless support is still unverified from the audit. Do not sign a fixed price for checkout until the gateway vendor confirms an API flow — carve it out as T&M if they cannot.
If they hesitate
Offer the staged route: ship the headless storefront now (₪80k), run it 6–12 months, migrate the back office to custom later only if the business case still holds. Lower risk, same end state, and you keep the relationship warm.
